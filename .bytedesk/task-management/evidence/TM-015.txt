test_launch.sh teardown race.

  test_launch.sh   OSError: [Errno 39] Directory not empty: 'home'

Its eight assertions had all passed; the teardown failed. test_launch.sh:7 used
tempfile.TemporaryDirectory as a context manager, and rmtree walked <root>/home
while a process spawned through the fake tmux was still writing into it.

Load-dependent: 4 of 4 standalone runs passed. It appeared only with a doc capture
and a node probe running on the same box - the worst shape of gate failure, since
it reads as "the launch tests broke" when nothing about launching broke.

Fixed with ignore_cleanup_errors=True (CPython added it in 3.10 for this; WSL here
is 3.12.3), with the reason recorded in the source so it is not later removed as
noise. Leaked files remain check_test_residue.sh's job, which has its own suite.

Three consecutive standalone runs green afterwards, and:
Gate 6 (clean run, ext4 clone, nothing else on the box):
