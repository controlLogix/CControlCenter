THE FIX. Three things now branch on suite type, and nothing else changed:
  1. the row regex accepts .sh or .py
  2. a .py suite runs as `python3 dashboard/<suite>` rather than through process
     substitution, which cannot work for python
  3. failures are counted with '^(FAIL|ERROR):' for python - unittest prints at
     column zero - and '^  FAIL' for shell

records that matching only one shape meant you could see THAT a suite broke and
never WHAT broke. Same reasoning, same two shapes.
