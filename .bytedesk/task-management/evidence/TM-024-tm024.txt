ONE INSTRUCTIVE REGRESSION. Registering the new check broke test_residue.sh
(32/3). Its fixture stubs the non-test_* suites from an explicit list -
smoke.sh and check_test_failability.sh - so a new one of those leaves the fixture
without the file and the runner fails on a missing script rather than on the
thing the case is testing. Added to that list, with a comment saying why, and the
suite is back to 35/0. Worth knowing: adding a gate suite means adding it there too.

A NOTE ON MY OWN PROCESS. The first attempt at this fix wrote a literal carriage
return into the tr argument instead of backslash-r, producing `tr -d ''` which
deletes nothing - the fourth time that escape collapsed on me in this session.
The repair builds the two characters from chr(92)+chr(114) and then verifies the
bytes are 0x5c 0x72, rather than trusting the write.
